# Venue checklist (do this before guests arrive)

Allow about 15 minutes. Tick each box.

## The day before (at home, online)
- [ ] Open the app URL in **Chrome** on the party phone and tap **Start**. Allow **camera** and **microphone**.
- [ ] Wait for **"✓ Ready for offline use"** (start screen, or ⚙ → App).
- [ ] Chrome **⋮ → Install app**. The DanceFigures icon is on the home screen.
- [ ] **Offline test:** close the app, turn on **Airplane mode**, open it from the home screen, tap Start. Figures appear. Turn Airplane mode off again.
- [ ] If you'll use the **Full** model, select it once while online and check that it loads (⚙ → Model → Full).
- [ ] Pack the **charger + long cable** and the **HDMI adapter** (or check that casting works with the venue TV/Chromecast), plus a **phone stand / tripod / tape**.

## At the venue
### Power and screen
- [ ] Phone **plugged in** and **battery saver OFF** (it slows the GPU and can block the screen-on lock).
- [ ] Do-not-disturb ON so calls and notifications don't pop up on the projector.
- [ ] Auto-rotate ON (or lock the phone to landscape).

### Output
- [ ] HDMI: plug in the adapter, and the phone screen appears on the projector. **or** Cast: Quick settings → Screen Cast → pick the TV.
- [ ] Open DanceFigures → **Start** → tap → **⛶ Fullscreen**. No browser bars or status bar on the projector.
- [ ] Laptop setup instead: ⚙ → **Open projector window** → drag it to the projector → click it / press **F**.

### Camera placement
- [ ] Phone in **landscape**, mounted **high** (head height or above), **3–6 m** from the dance floor, pointing at it.
- [ ] Check ⚙ → Camera says **now: back**. The app starts on the back camera; the front/selfie camera only sees whoever is holding the phone. Turn **Mirror** on in ⚙ if moving left should look like moving left on the projector.
- [ ] Turn on ⚙ → **Debug: show camera** to check the framing: whole bodies (at least head to hips) visible. Then turn Debug **off**.

### Detection
- [ ] Walk into the frame. A figure fades in within about a second and follows you.
- [ ] Two or three people dance: each has a **different color**, and colors don't swap when people cross.
- [ ] Walk out. Your figure fades away (it doesn't freeze or jump).
- [ ] **Lights:** with the party lighting on, figures still track. If not, add a steady light on the dancers or point strobes away from the camera.

### Performance
- [ ] ⚙ → **Show FPS counter** ON. With 3 people it shows **≥ 25–30 fps**. Check that ⚙ → Performance says **GPU** (not CPU).
- [ ] If it's below 20: set **People** to 2, keep the **Lite** model, try the **Stick** style. Turn the FPS counter off when you're done.

### Beat
- [ ] Music playing → ⚙ → the **beat meter** under Sensitivity jumps with the kick drum.
- [ ] Lines and background pulse to the beat. Adjust **Sensitivity** (too much pulsing → lower it; nothing → raise it).
- [ ] Mic blocked? Address-bar/App info → Permissions → Microphone → Allow, then reopen the app.

### Look
- [ ] Pick a **style** (swipe left/right on the screen), a **background** and **colors**. They're remembered next time.
- [ ] Leave it running for **5 minutes**: the screen doesn't dim or lock, and the phone isn't overheating. If it gets hot, take it out of its case and keep it out of direct sun or lights.

## If something goes wrong mid-party
| Problem | Quick fix |
|---|---|
| Frozen or black | Swipe the app away and reopen it → Start |
| Figures jittery | ⚙ → Smoothing up |
| Figures laggy | ⚙ → Smoothing down, or People → fewer |
| "Update available" popped up | Ignore it until after the party (it only applies when you tap Reload) |
| Camera "in use" | Close any other camera/video-call app → Retry |
